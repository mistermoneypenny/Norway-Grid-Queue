The question behind the queue

How fast does this queue actually move?

Statnett publishes a queue but no throughput. These three panels are computed from its own history of reservations, connections and withdrawals — they are the closest thing to an answer on when capacity frees up, and by which route.

Where this data comes from

Every figure on this page is either a Statnett field or computed here from one. Nothing is estimated.

Model
22622814 · dataset bb718231-fe68-48df-afd1-708303dbd8f0
Tables
Saker (active cases) · Tilknyttet kapasitet · Tilbaketrukket kapasitet saker · Stasjoner (substation lookup)
Snapshot
Coverage
Only cases with status queued, reserved or connected are published. Statnett has said it intends to publish all active cases later, so this is not the whole pipeline.
Accuracy
Statnett's records are manually registered from 2018 onward. Statnett states gaps occur. A change between snapshots can be a data-entry correction rather than real movement.
No ranking
Statnett publishes no ordinal queue position. Order is decided by project maturity, not seniority. Every “ahead” figure here is this dashboard's proxy, labelled as derived.
Price areas
NO1–NO5 is an administrative grouping. Real grid constraints cut across it, so a price-area total is not the bottleneck any one case sits behind.
Lefdal
Connects at 132 kV regional grid, so Linja AS holds the maturity assessment; Statnett handles only upstream capacity. Linja publishes no queue of its own.
Computed here
The queue-ahead proxy, waiting-time buckets, sector→colour mapping, cumulative series, and all change detection. None of these are Statnett figures.
