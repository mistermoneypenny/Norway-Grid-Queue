Kaplan-Meier · time from capacity queue to reservation

How long it actually takes

Statnett publishes no throughput figure. This estimates it from its own dates, counting the cases still waiting instead of quietly dropping them.

The obvious number is the wrong one

Taking the median wait across cases that reached a reservation gives a comfortable figure. It is survivorship bias: it can only see the cases that made it, and every case still stuck in the queue — the large majority — is excluded by construction. Censoring them properly instead gives a much longer answer.

Share still waiting, by time in the queue

Kaplan-Meier estimate with a Greenwood 95% band. Cases still in the queue are censored at their current elapsed time.

The band widens sharply past roughly two years because few cases have been in the queue that long. Treat the far right of this curve as a direction, not a date — the median is struck with a handful of cases still at risk.
